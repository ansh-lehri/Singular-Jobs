Frequently Asked Questions
What problem is Singular Jobs trying to solve ?

The idea of Seraching Jobs from different platforms, is what Singular Jobs is trying to solve.

How is Singular Jobs different from other job listing platforms ?

Unlike other platforms, Singular Jobs focuses on curating personalised job lists from different job platforms for its users so as to create a single gateway for apllying to jobs on different platforms.

What is vision of Singular Jobs ?

Singular Jobs aims at becoming a personalised gateway to jobs listed on different job boards/sites.

Why should I use Singular Jobs ?

Because of following points:

  • Single gateway to access jobs on different platforms.
  • Saves times spent on searching job opportunities.
  • Helps in discovering opportunities at new ventures.
  • Helps in applying to jobs as soon as possible.
  • Provides exposure to newer and newer job platforms, which normally would be difficult.

Is Singular Jobs an open-source project ?

No, Singular Jobs is not an open-source project.

Why some jobs recommended are completely out of my skill domain which I mentioned in my profile?

Singular Jobs try to give its user sense of diversity, dicovery and explorability. To achieve it, Singular Jobs predicts skills similar to your mentioned skills and uses these predicted skills as well to predict jobs for you. Say, a user mentioned ML, NLP, Python, Mongodb as skills, so Singular Jobs predicts AI, DL, Django, Web Dev, Maths as skills closely related and take them into account as well.

What are Singular Jobs sessions ?

1 Singular Jobs session = duration of 6 hours.

How much old jobs can I see on Singular Jobs ?

Jobs, 8-12 sessions old can be seen on Singular Jobs.

How many platforms does Singular Jobs utilise to curate job lists ?

3 platforms; Internshala, Naukri.com, GeeksGod are used for now.

Will more platforms and career pages be added in future ?

Definitely. Being a prototype, Singular Jobs is limited to 3 platforms but in some time more platforms can be added.

How can I improve my job list ?

Mention your skills in as detailed manner as possible with proper keywords.
